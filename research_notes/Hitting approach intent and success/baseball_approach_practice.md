## What artifacts exist and what fields do coaches fill in or read?

### Takeaway
Public evidence shows three artifact types: a pre-series pitcher/catcher meeting sheet that players can annotate (Red Sox, 2023), an advance scouting report plus "attack plan" sent to hitters (Orioles), and job-posting language that names "Advanced Reports and Game Plans" as an affiliate hitting coach deliverable. Concrete field lists for hitter-facing one-pagers are thin in public sources.

### Cited Findings
- Red Sox pitching coach, pitchers and catchers meet before the first game of a series. They go over hitters' results against RHP and LHP, performance against pitch types, 0-0 count tendencies, and where each hitter is prone to swing and miss. Players get a sheet with these details and can take notes on it. Tier B, secondary summary of a paywalled article, not fully read. — [Boston Globe, 2023-07-03](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/07/03/sports/an-inside-look-how-red-sox-game-plan-attack-opposing-hitters); related: [Boston Globe, 2023-05-04, hitters meetings before game](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2023/05/04/sports/red-sox-arent-just-playing-good-game-theyre-talking-good-one-too-hitters-meetings-before-game)
- Orioles hitters get the advance scouting report and possible attack plans from hitting coaches by WhatsApp group message the night before. After batting practice they meet coaches individually about plate approach. The club dropped large group hitting meetings. Utilityman Blaze Alexander is the named source for the WhatsApp detail. Manager Craig Albernaz said some players sat in the back of group meetings and did not speak up. Tier B, from search summary of The Banner, article date n/a (circa 2025). — [The Banner](https://www.thebanner.com/sports/orioles-mlb/orioles-hitting-meetings-individual-approach-FLG3E7UP2REEBFCGRM3PRC5VSI/)
- Red Sox "Affiliate Hitting Coach" posting lists: "Deliver Advanced Reports and Game Plans to hitters"; operate Blast Motion, HitTrax, Trajekt and "internal software"; lead the hitting portion of Player Performance Meetings; help carry out "individual player plans". Tier B/C (primary employer document, posting date n/a). — [Red Sox Lever posting](https://jobs.lever.co/redsox/edc90b80-7e24-4253-bd62-b0a1d352e9a0)
- A separate job-board snippet says minor league hitting coaches scout opposing starters' recent outings and compare them with the scouting report to spot tendencies. Tier C/D, aggregator snippet. — [Miami Marlins minor league hitting coach posting](https://www.teamworkonline.com/baseball-jobs/miamibaseball/miami-marlins/minor-league-hitting-coach-2184897)
- A Johns Hopkins design-day poster describes a prototype "SLUGGER Batter Flashcard" tablet widget. It surfaces "Attack" and "Vulnerable" zones per batter and was built because traditional scouting reports are text-heavy. This is a student prototype, not club practice. Tier A-/D (unreviewed poster). — [JHU poster PDF](https://engineering.jhu.edu/designcenter/wp-content/uploads/formidable/32/SARG-SLUGGER-Batter-Flashcard-Design-Day-Poster_compressed.pdf)
- Dave Magadan (then Diamondbacks hitting coach) described himself as an "offensive coordinator" building a game plan for hitters and said he tried to "simplify the scouting report". His advance reports noted specific pitch and velocity locations that hitters fouled off or missed. Tier B, search summary, date n/a. — [Arizona Sports](https://arizonasports.com/mlb/arizona-diamondbacks/d-backs-hitting-coach-magadan-gm-hazen-discuss-teams-hitting-approach)

### Inferences
- The only concrete public "fields" are pitcher-side and hitter-tendency stats (platoon splits, pitch-type results, 0-0 tendencies, whiff zones). A hitter-intent field (what to hunt, what to do with it) was not found in any public template.
- "Game plan" and "attack plan" appear to be the common nouns for the hitter-facing deliverable. Both appear in club postings or named-player quotes.

### Gaps
- No actual hitter one-pager, dugout card or pregame packet image or template from a pro club was found. Full text of Banner, Globe and Hudl pages was not read (fetch blocked).
- No Saberseminar, SABR, or MIT Sloan paper on this specific topic was found. SABR's Dana Levangie piece ("Every Game is a Road Game", sabr.org/journal) surfaced in search but was not read, so its relevance is unconfirmed.

## What approach vocabulary is used, and how standard is it?

### Takeaway
Terms like "hunt" (zone or pitch), "two-strike approach", "attack plan", "game plan" and "approach" recur across named sources, but no published taxonomy was found. Meanings appear to be set per coach or club.

### Cited Findings
- Nationals minor league hitting coordinator Troy Gingrich required in 2016 that every hitter in the system have "a plan and a two-strike approach", at minimum choking up. Juan Soto's two-strike stance is cited as an outcome. Tier B, MLB.com (Phillies site). — [MLB.com](https://www.mlb.com/phillies/news/how-juan-soto-developed-his-two-strike-batting-stance)
- The two-strike approach as taught: "late and on top" (let the ball travel deeper, keep the bat above the ball's center). Tier D, coaching-site article, date n/a. — [Coaches Insider](https://coachesinsider.com/baseball/hitting-less-than-two-strikes-and-with-two-strikes-article)
- Hitting instructors define approach as "an adjustment and strategy", meaning what can be done in the moment to succeed. Tier B/D, from a ranked search summary, speaker not confirmed. — [Dallas News, 2019-02-05, Rangers two-strike work](https://sportsday.dallasnews.com/texas-rangers/rangers/2019/02/05/rangers-spring-training-projectsimproving-two-strike-approach-plate)
- "Hunt" language: a hunter's "game plan is nothing more than his intent, what he is looking for, and what he plans to do with the pitch." Tier D, apparel-brand blog (Warstic), date n/a. — [Warstic](https://warstic.com/blogs/warriors-library/the-hunter-vs-the-hunted)
- A Driveline-attributed description says hitters use a scouting report to learn how the pitcher attacks similar hitters, his out pitch, and which pitches he is landing. It also says players get automated reports on whether they followed the game plan. This came via a search summary that blended sources, so the Driveline attribution is not verified. Tier C/D. — [search hit: Driveline philosophy piece, 2026-09-04](https://fantasynerds.com/news/story/2026/09/04/drivelines-hitting-philosophy-a-double-edged-sword-in-mlb-development-1615174)
- Magadan's "tunneling" is described by him as "probably just another word to get a good pitch to hit". This suggests vocabulary is partly rebranding. Tier B, date n/a. — [Arizona Sports](https://arizonasports.com/mlb/arizona-diamondbacks/d-backs-hitting-coach-magadan-gm-hazen-discuss-teams-hitting-approach)
- Diamondbacks under Magadan: attack early in counts and improve the two-strike approach. Tier B, same source.

### Inferences
- Count-based labels (first pitch, early count, two-strike) are the most widely shared vocabulary. Zone and pitch "hunt" terms are common but defined differently by each source.
- Because even the same word ("tunneling") is called a rename by its user, a tool should use plain, editable labels instead of enforcing a fixed taxonomy.

### Gaps
- No published taxonomy, glossary or inter-club consistency study found. "Take until strike", "work the count" and "situational approach" were not located in a named-source context in this pass.

## What tools capture intent or plan, and how is the plan handed to the player?

### Takeaway
Tools found capture results and tendencies (video tags, pitch data, swing metrics), not the planned intent. Delivery is mostly words (one-on-one meetings), plus digital messages and sheets. Public info on vendor fields for "plan" is limited.

### Cited Findings
- Hudl Sportscode has a baseball code window that records the result of every pitch and hit, with automated situational and matchup labels per pitch, plus iPad access in the dugout for in-game adjustments and multi-game opposition scouting databases. Tier C, vendor blog, date n/a. — [Hudl blog](https://hudl.com/blog/dominate-the-diamond-with-hudl-sportscode)
- Hudl hitter charts and tendencies content (spray charts, pitch-type contact quality). Tier C. — [Hudl blog on overlooked tendencies](https://www.hudl.com/blog/the-most-overlooked-tendencies-that-make-a-difference-in-scouting)
- Named tools in pro postings: Blast Motion, HitTrax, Trajekt, internal software (Red Sox); TrackMan, Blast, KinaTrax, Edgertronic (a job-board snippet describing minor league coaching). Tier B/C. — [Red Sox posting](https://jobs.lever.co/redsox/edc90b80-7e24-4253-bd62-b0a1d352e9a0); [Marlins posting](https://www.teamworkonline.com/baseball-jobs/miamibaseball/miami-marlins/minor-league-hitting-coach-2184897)
- Delivery formats: WhatsApp message of scouting report and attack plan, then one-on-one meeting (Orioles); annotated paper or digital sheet in a group meeting (Red Sox pitching side). Tier B (sources above).
- A Substack practitioner post argues a three-minute glance at the report is not enough and hitters should be able to say how they will attack that pitcher that day. Tier D. — [jrod20033 Substack](https://jrod20033.substack.com/p/feeling-ready-isnt-being-ready)

### Inferences
- A hitter-intent field (hunt zone/pitch, count plan, two-strike plan) captured before the game and compared with swings afterward would not duplicate what the named tools do. This is an inference from absence in the sources, not a confirmed market gap.

### Gaps
- Rapsodo, Hawk-Eye, Synergy and TrackMan documentation on plan or intent fields was not searched or found. No vendor help-center page showing a "plan" field was located.

## Pitch-calling analogs (catcher game plans, PitchCom, pitcher plan sheets)

### Takeaway
The pitching side has a documented pre-series plan sheet and an electronic delivery channel (PitchCom). The hitter side has no equivalent standard channel found.

### Cited Findings
- PitchCom: the catcher presses buttons for pitch type and location; the pitcher hears a spoken English or Spanish cue and can shake off. Introduced to speed play and counter sign stealing. Tier B/C, news and vendor, 2022. — [MLB.com on Rays](https://www.mlb.com/news/rays-praise-pitchcom-electronic-pitch-calling-device); [Voicebot, 2022-03-28](https://voicebot.ai/2022/03/28/mlb-tests-electronic-voice-transmitter-for-catchers-to-signal-pitchers/)
- The vendor says calls can come from the catcher or a coach in the dugout and that catchers can "trust their scouting report". Tier C, marketing. — [PitchCom blog](https://pitchcom.com/blogs/pitchcom%C2%AE-blog/pace-meets-precision)
- Red Sox pitcher/catcher meeting sheet: see first section. Astros' game-planning is called "masterful" in a Boston Sports Journal column (Tier D, 2021-10-13), no detail. — [BSJ](https://www.bostonsportsjournal.com/2021/10/13/Red-Sox-will-have-to-overcome-Astros-masterful-gameplanning-alcs)

### Inferences
- The pitching analog suggests the unit of planning is the series and the opposing lineup, with the sheet annotated by players. A hitter tool could mirror that: plan per opposing starter, annotated by the hitter.

### Gaps
- Not found: a pitcher's own game-plan sheet fields, catcher card contents, or any public description of in-game hitter signaling beyond ordinary signs.

## What do minor league player-development staff say about written versus verbal approach?

### Takeaway
No direct public statement on written versus verbal approach at the Single-A level was found. Indirect evidence: organizations like the Nationals mandate that each hitter "have a plan", and Orioles staff moved from group to individual conversation while still sending written reports.

### Cited Findings
- Gingrich mandate (plan plus two-strike approach for every hitter): see vocabulary section. — [MLB.com](https://www.mlb.com/phillies/news/how-juan-soto-developed-his-two-strike-batting-stance)
- Orioles: written report by message, verbal individual meeting after BP; manager says individual format removes hiding in the back. — [The Banner](https://www.thebanner.com/sports/orioles-mlb/orioles-hitting-meetings-individual-approach-FLG3E7UP2REEBFCGRM3PRC5VSI/)
- Red Sox posting: affiliate coaches deliver reports and game plans and track "individual player plans", which implies a written artifact exists at the affiliate level for that club. Tier B/C, and "implies" is my reading. — [Red Sox posting](https://jobs.lever.co/redsox/edc90b80-7e24-4253-bd62-b0a1d352e9a0)

### Inferences
- Written reports are a pre-game input, while commitment to a specific plan is made verbally. A tool might add value by recording the hitter's own stated plan, but no source tested this.

### Gaps
- No named Single-A or California League staff interview or podcast on written versus verbal approach found. Podcast and conference-slide searches (Saberseminar, Sloan) were not run in this pass.
