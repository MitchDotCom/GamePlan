Reading (the first four blocks reproduce docs/traits_test.txt exactly; the baseline throughout is the full kernel model, ContactModel shapecount with his hitter term):
- Plane fit passes against the full kernel on both seasons. PLANE: +0.00314 log-loss per swing in 2025 [+0.00186, +0.00447] and +0.00229 in 2024 [+0.00128, +0.00334]. The verdict-label gate fixed in the docstring is met.
- Most of that is the pitch-conditional angle, not the hitter. PLANE_LG (league curve at the league-expected angle, no hitter in it) gives +0.00243 and +0.00178. The hitter-specific part, his expected angle instead of the league's, adds +0.00072 [+0.00021, +0.00122] in 2025 and +0.00052 [+0.00003, +0.00096] in 2024. Real, both seasons, small.
- His personal curve shape adds nothing once his expected angle is in: +0.00041 [-0.00007, +0.00088] in 2025 and +0.00015 [-0.00029, +0.00060] in 2024. The earlier PERSONAL pass is mostly this same signal, so the model to carry forward is the league curve at his expected angle.
- Damage still fails everywhere (every block, both seasons). Nothing here supports changing a damage call. PATH and SPEED alone still fail on whiffs, so they stay a descriptive card.
- WS1.3 check (not adopted): a richer league trait model with pitch and count interactions lowers held-out trait error by 3.2% for bat speed, 0.6% for attack angle, 0.5% for swing length, 0.3% for tilt, against a spread dominated by who the hitter is. Not worth changing the shipped model.
- Not done: squared-up and exit velocity as separate outcomes. They need a kernel-grade baseline; a weak baseline would let pitch shape masquerade as swing information. Held until the damage outcome gets a baseline of its own.
