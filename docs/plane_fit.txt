Reading (written after the run; gate and models were fixed before it, one change noted below):
- The U shape survives controls. With pitch family, velocity, location, count, approach angle, break and a hitter intercept held fixed, whiffs are lowest at 12 to 24 degrees VBA
  and rise steeply past 24 (about 0.18 at 18-24, 0.29 at 24-30, 0.49 at 30-36). Controlling flattens the extremes (0.83 raw becomes 0.67 at 42+), so the raw table overstated the tails.
- The gated result is the pre-pitch expected angle (M2): +0.0028 log-loss per swing in 2025 [+0.0021, +0.0035] and +0.0015 in 2024 [+0.0006, +0.0024]. Both intervals exclude zero, so the verdict-label gate passes.
- Size: small. M1 (realized angle, +0.028) is about 10 times larger, but the realized angle is partly the hitter reacting to the pitch and timing, so it is a shape description, not a forecast.
- The first run omitted approach angle and break from the controls and gave larger gains (+0.0033 and +0.0026). I added them as controls before relying on any result, because VBA contains the approach angle and the gain could have been pitch shape alone. Gains fell about 15 to 40% and still pass. That edit is the only change after seeing a result.
- Limits: the baseline M0 is a simple logistic model, not the full kernel model that the plan uses; a pass here does not prove the angle adds beyond the kernel. The traits test (traits_test.py, PERSONAL block) already did that against the kernel and passed. Timing (contact point) is not controlled; those fields are not in our downloads. Contact quality is not scored here.
